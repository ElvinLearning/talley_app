# Anthony's demo walkthrough

Use the local demo accounts described in the README. No real client records or
prices are required. The sample starts as a draft so approval can be demonstrated live.

1. Sign in as **admin.demo**. In Jobs, open **Demo · South Philadelphia kitchen refresh**.
   Review Jordan Example's contact, the synthetic site and scope. To demonstrate
   intake instead, select **New job**, enter a new customer (or choose an existing
   customer), site and scope, then create the job.
2. Open **Revision 1**. There are two sections and three editable lines:

   | Item | Quantity | Unit cost | Cost | Selling price at 20% markup |
   | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
   | Installation labor | 8 hours | $65.00 | $520.00 | $624.00 |
   | Tile allowance | 100 sq ft | $3.25 | $325.00 | $390.00 |
   | Electrical subcontract | 1 allowance | $250.00 | $250.00 | $300.00 |

   Cost is **$1,095.00**, markup **$219.00**, selling subtotal **$1,314.00**,
   illustrative materials tax **$23.40**, total **$1,337.40**.
3. Change a quantity or rate, **Save estimate**, refresh the browser, reopen the
   job and draft, and verify the saved value. Restore the sample value if desired.
   Unsaved edits disable preview/approval and prompt before leaving the estimate.
4. Open **Client preview**. It shows the customer, site, scope, quantities and
   selling totals; internal costs and markup are absent. Use Print / save PDF if
   desired, then return. No proposal is emailed.
5. Enter **Jordan Example** and **Synthetic approval during demo** in the approval
   fields. Select **Record approval & lock revision**. The revision becomes read-only.
6. Select **Open / create project from approval**. The project carries the customer,
   site, scope, $1,095 cost budget and $1,337.40 contract total. Return to the same
   approved estimate and repeat conversion: it opens the same project.
7. Under **Field access**, check **Demo field worker**, then save access. Under
   Kitchen preparation & tile select **+ Task**. Add **Measure kitchen**, choose
   **Demo field worker**, set a due date, and save. Jobs now shows this next action.
8. Sign out and sign in as **worker.demo**. Open the assigned job, change the task
   status and add a note. Save and refresh. No internal financial fields are shown.
   **other.demo** sees no jobs until explicitly assigned.
9. Sign in again as **admin.demo** and inspect the saved task and activity.
10. Back on the job, **Create revision**. Change estimated scope or pricing and
    save. Revision 1 remains approved and unchanged. The existing project's
    original scope/budget remains frozen; a new revision is not an automatic change order.
